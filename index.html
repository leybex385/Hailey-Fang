Meet The Designer

Hailey Fang

Hailey Fang

U.S. Regional Director

Hailey Fang founded NOT JUST A LABEL in 2014 as a startup fashion brand in China, with a clear vision to create modern women's fashion that bridges Eastern heritage and Western sophistication. Her passion for design and commitment to quality quickly propelled the brand into the international spotlight.

Graduating from Tsinghua University's prestigious Design Major, Hailey developed a keen eye for detail and a deep appreciation for both traditional craftsmanship and contemporary aesthetics. This unique educational background became the foundation for her distinctive design approach that would later define the brand's identity.

Throughout her career, Hailey has led design direction and developed multiple seasonal collections, each telling a unique story through fabric, silhouette, and detail. Her hands-on approach to every aspect of the business—from initial sketch to final garment—ensures that each piece meets her exacting standards of excellence.

Based in Los Angeles, Hailey manages end-to-end business operations including design, sourcing, and production. She has established strong supplier relationships across Asia and Europe, managed small-batch production to maintain exclusivity, and developed robust online sales channels and brand marketing strategies that connect NOT JUST A LABEL directly with discerning customers worldwide.

Operations & Excellence

End-to-End Excellence

From concept to creation, we manage every aspect of the fashion journey with meticulous attention to detail. Our integrated approach ensures uncompromising quality at every stage of production, from initial sketch to final garment.

Since founding NOT JUST A LABEL in 2014, Hailey Fang has cultivated lasting partnerships with premium suppliers across Asia and Europe, ensuring access to the finest materials and craftsmanship. Our small-batch production model allows us to maintain exclusivity while meeting demand, with each piece receiving individual attention from our skilled artisans.

Through strategic online sales channels and innovative brand marketing initiatives, we connect directly with discerning customers worldwide. Our Los Angeles headquarters serves as the creative hub where Eastern heritage meets Western sophistication, resulting in collections that transcend seasonal trends and speak to the modern woman who values both tradition and innovation.
